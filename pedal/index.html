WATT DUNGEON

A Diablo-lite you pedal · engine demo with simulated power & cadence
  • Your hero explores and fights automatically. You choose the path, so you choose how hard the day is.
  • Any riding banks kilojoules that charge Cleave. Easy rides still progress.
  • Sweet spot held builds Strength. Cadence sets attack speed and picks treasure locks.
  • Above FTP charges Overload and adds crit chance, but drains your W′ stamina.
  • Enemies telegraph moves you answer with your legs: SURGE to dodge charges and bombs, EASE OFF to brace slams, SPIN to interrupt casters, HOLD sweet spot to outlast a frenzy.
  • Your zone sets your element: ❄ Frost (easy to tempo), 🔥 Fire (sweet spot to threshold), ⚡ Storm (VO2 and sprint). Hit a weakness for double damage.
  • Know your foes: shield bearers block all but crits, healers mend allies, archers keep their distance, bats swarm (Cleave them).
  • Campfires heal faster when you spin easy. Dying costs some gold, never the run.
