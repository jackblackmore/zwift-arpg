WATT DUNGEON

ARPG prototype · Path of Exile style · bike controls come later · original pedal demo · asset gallery
Characters, animations and dungeon: KayKit by Kay Lousberg (CC0)
  • Mow down hordes. Fireball auto-attacks (elites first), Frost Nova freezes, Ward absorbs damage, Blink jumps you away from danger. No mana: skills run on cooldowns.
  • Hunt rare monsters (yellow names) with affixes like Hasted, Splitting or Volatile.
  • Loot drops with beams. Magic (blue), rare (yellow) and unique (orange) gear with affixes that change your skills.
  • Level up and pick passives to build: shatter, ignite, cooldown reduction, more fire.
  • FX switches graphics quality (high: bloom + shadows, medium: bloom, low: plain) if your device struggles.
  • Fight through the crypt. Beats (elites, ambushes, survival, treasure, gambles) lie along a route and end at a guardian. Kill it and step through the portal for a fresh level. Progress saves on this device.
Desktop: move WASD · aim with mouse · Fireball fires on its own (hold LMB or 1 to aim it by hand) · Q/RMB Frost Nova · E Ward · Space Blink · F flask · I bag · P passives
Touch: drag the left side to move · Fireball fires on its own; buttons on the right cast Nova, Ward and Blink · walk over loot to pick it up
RIDE mode (MODE button) plays with only what a Zwift Ride + KICKR give you: pedal to move (power slider, W/S; cadence A/D), D-pad steers (arrows), Fireball fires on its own, A Frost Nova K, B Ward L, Y autopilot I, right shifter Blink E, left shifter flask Q.
