WATT DUNGEON

ARPG prototype · Path of Exile style · bike controls come later · original pedal demo · asset gallery
Characters, animations and dungeon: KayKit by Kay Lousberg (CC0)
  • Start with one skill. Pick Fireball, Frost Lance or Raise Skeletons at the first shrine; Magic Shield soaks up hits. Shrines at levels 3, 5, 8 and 12 add more skills: Frost Ward, Arc, Frost Nova, Flame Totem, Meteor, Flame Dash and Blink.
  • Skill gems level up as you kill (up to 10) and change at level 5: Fireball pierces, Arc chains further, Nova grows. Swap them with G.
  • Hunt rare monsters (yellow names) with affixes like Hasted, Splitting or Volatile.
  • Loot drops with beams. Magic (blue), rare (yellow) and unique (orange) gear with affixes that change your skills.
  • Level up and pick passives to build: chain lightning, shatter, ignite, Blood Magic.
  • FX switches graphics quality (high: bloom + shadows, medium: bloom, low: plain) if your device struggles.
  • Kill the map boss and step through the portal into a harder map. Progress saves on this device.
Desktop: move WASD · aim with mouse · skill bar: LMB Main · RMB Flex A · Q Flex B · Space Guard · E Evade (or 1–5) · F flask · I bag · G gems · P passives
Touch: drag the left side to move · skills on the right auto-aim at the nearest enemy · walk over loot to pick it up
RIDE mode (MODE button) plays with only what a Zwift Ride + KICKR give you: pedal to move (power slider, W/S; cadence A/D), D-pad steers (arrows), A Main K, B Flex A L, Z Flex B J, Y autopilot I, right shifter Guard E, left power-up button Evade U, left shifter flask Q. Harder pedalling refills mana faster; high cadence casts faster.
