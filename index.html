WATT DUNGEON

ARPG prototype · Path of Exile style · bike controls come later · original pedal demo · asset gallery
Characters, animations and dungeon: KayKit by Kay Lousberg (CC0)
  • Mow down hordes. Your Main skill auto-attacks, going for elites first, then the nearest enemy in range. The Flex, Guard and Evade skills run on cooldowns. No mana.
  • Clear the crypt beat by beat. Each level is a run of packs, treasure or blood altars, elites, ambushes, survival fights and loot caches. Every beat shows a beacon, a banner when you near it and a short wind-up before it starts.
  • Kill the guardian to clear the level, then step through the portal for a fresh one. Dying respawns you at the last beat you finished. Progress saves on this device.
  • Gems make the skill bar. Skills are gems in your gem bag (GEMS button or G). Five slots (Main, Flex A, Flex B, Guard, Evade) unlock as you level (Flex A at 5, Guard at 8, Flex B at 12); each has sockets for support gems that change the skill beside them. Swap gems any time.
  • Gems level with use. A gem earns experience from kills and time while it is on the bar or socketed (others earn a tenth). Higher levels change how the skill plays, like Fireball piercing or leaving burning ground.
  • Hunt rare monsters (yellow names) with affixes like Hasted, Splitting or Volatile.
  • Loot drops with beams. Magic (blue), rare (yellow) and unique (orange) gear with affixes that change your skills.
  • Level up for a passive pick (a placeholder for now).
  • FX switches graphics quality (high: bloom + shadows, medium: bloom, low: plain) if your device struggles.
Desktop: move WASD or arrows · aim with mouse · Main fires on its own (hold LMB or 1 to fire it at the cursor yourself) · Q/RMB Flex A · R Flex B · E Guard · Space Evade · F flask · I item bag · G gems · P passive tree
Touch: drag the left side to move · Main fires on its own; buttons on the right cast Flex A, Flex B, Guard and Evade (tap an empty slot to open the gem bag) · walk over loot to pick it up
RIDE mode (MODE button) plays with only what a Zwift Ride + KICKR give you. Pedalling past about 35% FTP moves you; below that you walk slowly or stand still. Power sets Main's attack speed, and riding above endurance (about 70% FTP) drains the Surge gauge for faster attacks until it runs out. Keyboard stand-ins: power W/S, cadence D/A, D-pad arrows, A Flex A K, Z Flex B J, B Guard L, Y autopilot I, right shifter Evade E, left shifter flask Q.
