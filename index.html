How to play
  • You're a knight: fight up close. Your sword attacks on its own whatever's in reach (the ATTACK button or T switches to hold-to-attack). The skill is in the timing, not where you stand.
  • Your shield (Guard, Space): hold to block hits from the front; press just before a hit lands to parry: no damage, the attacker staggers and your next swing is a riposte. Blocking fills your posture bar (over your head); fill it and your guard breaks. The level 3 shrine offers a shield art for Guard: Magic Shield (a parry wraps you in a barrier) or Frost Ward (a parry chills the attacker and leaves a frost ring).
  • Roll (Evade, E): untouchable for a moment. Yellow flash: parry it. Red ground: block it for less or roll for none. Purple: roll, a block won't hold. Blue bar on the guardian: break its stagger (parry, hit while surging) before it fills. Every cue has its own sound.
  • Stagger guardians and rares: parries fill the bar under their life (hits a little; a hit they land on you knocks it back). Full, they're stunned and your first hit is a finisher for four times the damage.
  • Shrines at levels 3, 5, 8 and 12 add skills: a shield art (Frost Ward or Magic Shield), Frost Nova, Arc, Meteor, Flame Totem, the melee gems, Flame Dash and Blink.
  • Skill gems level up as you kill (up to 10) and change at level 5: Cleave swings wider, Arc chains further, Nova grows. Swap them with G.
  • Hunt rare monsters (yellow names) with affixes like Hasted, Splitting or Volatile.
  • Loot drops with beams. Magic (blue), rare (yellow) and unique (orange) gear with affixes that change your skills.
  • Support gems go in the sockets under a skill and change only that skill: Sweep, Brittle, Aftershock, Ignite, Raise Skeletons (your kills rise and fight for you), Forked Sky, Shatter and more. Guardians always drop a gem, rares sometimes. Feed spare gems into the ones you use.
  • No mana. Skills are limited by cast time and cooldowns. Hold Shift (on touch, tap ⚡ on and off) to Surge: everything swings, casts and recovers faster until the gauge (the gold orb) runs dry. Save it for rares and guardians.
  • Passive tree (P or TREE): every level gives a point. Take nodes next to ones you hold: life and Surge in the middle, the shield above (parry window, riposte, block and posture, roll, finishers), fire, cold and lightning below. Four keystones change the rules at a price: Riposte, Bulwark, Duelist and Blood Rite. A full reset is free in the room you enter a map by or at a rest room's flame.
  • FX switches graphics quality (high: bloom + shadows, medium: bloom, low: plain) if your device struggles.
  • Kill the map boss and step through the portal into a harder map. Progress saves on this device.
Controls
Desktop: move WASD · aim with mouse · skill bar: LMB Main (hold to swing at the cursor) · T auto-attack on/off · RMB Flex A · Q Flex B · Space Guard · E Evade (or 1–5) · Shift (hold) Surge · F flask · I bag · G gems · P passive tree
Touch: drag the left side to move (moving away from a monster pauses the auto-attack) · skills on the right auto-aim at the nearest enemy · walk over loot to pick it up
RIDE mode
RIDE mode (MODE button) plays with only what a Zwift Ride + KICKR give you: pedal to move (power slider, W/S; cadence A/D), D-pad steers (arrows), A Main K, B Flex A L, Z Flex B J, Y autopilot I (walks into the fight; the Main attack swings on its own; when a cleared room has several ways on it waits 5 s for you to tap the D-pad toward one, else it clears everything), right shifter Guard E, left power-up button Evade U, left shifter flask Q. There is no mana: ride above endurance pace (~80% FTP) to Surge (faster casts and cooldowns while the gauge drains), ease off to refill it; below ~65% FTP casts slow down; high cadence casts a little faster.
